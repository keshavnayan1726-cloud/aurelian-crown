Essential Home Rituals

The 5 Rules of Hair Patch Longevity

Follow these four simple daily practices developed by master stylists Ajay Thakur & Bittu Thakur.

1

Gentle Downward Washing

Always wash in the shower with water flowing down your face and neck. Use lukewarm water and sulphate-free shampoo. Smooth gently downwards; never rub in vigorous circular motions which causes tangles.

2

Conditioning the Strands Only

Apply nourishing argan or keratin conditioner to the mid-lengths and ends of the hair. Avoid applying conditioner directly to the base membrane, as silicone oils can soften the bonding adhesive.

3

Pat Dry & Wide-Tooth Comb

Gently press and pat the hair with a soft microfiber towel. Never wring or twist. Detangle gently using a wide-toothed comb starting from the tips and working your way up to the roots.

4

Bedtime Friction Prevention

Sleep on a satin or silk pillowcase instead of coarse cotton. Silk reduces hair friction, prevents knot breakage, preserves hair moisture, and extends the life of your styled hairline.

5

Helmet & Gym Protection

Wear a thin breathable skullcap or bandana under your motorcycle helmet. After intense gym sessions or sweating, rinse the hairline with cool water and gently pat dry.

6

20–25 Day Studio Servicing

Visit our Sector 3A Laxman Vihar studio for routine residue breakdown, scalp cleansing, hair deep conditioning, and fresh medical re-bonding in under 45 minutes.

What YOU SHOULD DO

  • Use gentle sulphate-free and paraben-free shampoos.
  • Apply a light leave-in conditioner spray with UV protection.
  • Blow-dry on cool or warm settings, keeping dryer 6 inches away.
  • Wear a swim cap or rinse immediately after chlorinated pool swimming.
  • Schedule routine servicing every 3–4 weeks before adhesives soften.

What YOU MUST AVOID

  • Never scrub the top base aggressively like a natural scalp wash.
  • Never use boiling hot water, which loosens medical tapes.
  • Do not apply hair oils (mustard, coconut) directly onto the bond.
  • Avoid sleeping with soaking wet hair systems.
  • Do not attempt to rip or pull off the system without proper solvent.
