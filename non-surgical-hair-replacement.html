How Non-Surgical Hair Replacement Works

Unlike surgical procedures that extract follicles from your donor zone with invasive punches, non-surgical replacement custom-fits a breathable, dermatologically safe micro-system to your exact baldness pattern.

1

Custom Scalp Mapping

Ajay & Bittu assess your skull curvature, temple contours, and crown hair swirl pattern. We calibrate the hair strand thickness and natural color gradient.

2

Dermal Adhesion

The breathable base is bonded using medical-grade, hypoallergenic, sweat-proof dermatological polymers or skin tapes that let your scalp pores breathe freely.

3

Styling & Blending

We perform precision razor-cut texturizing so the system merges undetectably into your existing hair on the sides and back, creating a 360-degree seamless flow.

Advantages of Non-Surgical Systems

  • Instant Transformation: Walk in with hair loss, walk out 60 minutes later with full, rich hair density.
  • 100% Pain-Free: No needles, no anesthesia, zero cuts, and zero blood loss.
  • Guaranteed Density: Achieve thick, youthful hair volume regardless of donor area limitations.
  • Fully Reversible: You maintain total control; change hair styles, lengths, or colors anytime.
  • Affordable Investment: Costs start from ₹3,000 to ₹12,000—a fraction of surgical clinic bills.

Frustrations with Surgical Transplants

  • Pain & Invasive Recovery: Scalp swelling, bloody crusting, and weeks of painful post-op care.
  • 6 to 12 Months Waiting: Grafts shed in shock loss; results take up to an entire year to show.
  • Limited Donor Supply: Severe baldness (Norwood 5–7) cannot achieve full crown density from surgery.
  • Risk of Failure: Grafts can fail to take root, leaving patchy see-through areas and permanent donor scars.
  • High Upfront Cost: Costs typically range from ₹60,000 to ₹2,50,000+ with no guarantee of full coverage.
Attachment Methods

3 Ways We Bond Your System Securely

Tailored to your lifestyle, activity level, and preference for home care vs studio servicing.

Liquid Medical Adhesive Bonding

Our most popular studio method. A hypoallergenic water-based or acrylic copolymer adhesive creates a secure 20–30 day hold that withstands heavy sweating, swimming, and helmet use.

  • Hold Time: 3 to 4 Weeks
  • Removal: In-Studio Servicing
  • Activity: Gym, Swimming, Motorbikes

Medical Contour Tapes

High-tack double-sided dermatological tapes (Ultra Hold, Supertape) applied along the polyurethane border. Gentle on sensitive scalps and easy for at-home touch-ups.

  • Hold Time: 2 to 3 Weeks
  • Removal: Easy DIY / Studio
  • Activity: Office, Daily Travel, Cardio

Micro-Clip Attachment

For clients who prefer removing their hair system before sleep. Small silicone-coated micro-clips anchor securely into your surrounding natural hair without any shaving.

  • Hold Time: Daily On / Off
  • Removal: Instant (Under 10 Seconds)
  • Activity: Occasional events, Weddings, Office
