Ethical Advisory & Medical Disclosure

Aurelian Crown Hair Patch Studio specializes exclusively in non-surgical, cosmetic hair replacement using 100% human hair systems, dermal bonding, and breathable lace bases. We do not perform surgical hair transplant operations or invasive medical procedures. If you are considering surgical options, we encourage you to consult board-certified plastic surgeons and dermatologists to evaluate your scalp suitability.

When is a Hair Patch the Right Choice?

  • You have advanced hair loss (Norwood Stage 4, 5, 6, or 7) with limited donor hair.
  • You need an immediate, flawless transformation for an upcoming wedding, interview, or social milestone.
  • You do not want scalp surgery, surgical incisions, or medical downtime.
  • You desire thick, dense hair rather than a sparse, see-through hairline.
  • You want complete control to adjust hairline design, density, and style over time.

When is a Hair Transplant Considered?

  • You have minor, localized thinning with a dense, genetically permanent donor bank at the back of your head.
  • You are comfortable paying ₹1,00,000+ for surgical hospital facilities.
  • You are prepared to wait 9 to 12 months for dormant follicles to mature.
  • You are willing to take ongoing prescription medications (like Finasteride) to protect untreated natural follicles.
